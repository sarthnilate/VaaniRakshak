**Document Purpose:** Complete audit trail of training, evaluation, and benchmark audio datasets used in VaaniRakshak anti-spoofing, speaker verification, and STT pipelines.

## 2. Dataset Split & Anti-Leakage Protocol

To ensure strict scientific integrity and prevent data leakage:
- **Speaker-Disjoint Split**: No speaker present in the training set appears in the validation or held-out testing set ($0\%$ overlap).
- **Generator-Disjoint Evaluation**: Validation sets include synthetic samples generated by algorithms completely unseen during model training (e.g. training on Tacotron/WaveNet, testing on Bark/StyleTTS2/ElevenLabs).
- **Telecom Degradation Augmentation**: 40% of test split is passed through the `DegradationSimulator` (AMR-WB 12.65kbps compression, G.711 $\mu$-law, 10dB urban background noise).
