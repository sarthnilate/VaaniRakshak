## 1. Threat Model & Adversarial Vectors

| Threat Vector | Attack Scenario | Defensive Countermeasure |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice Cloning Impersonation** | Scammer uses AI voice clone of family member to demand urgent funds. | Combined ECAPA-TDNN high speaker similarity + WavLM high synthetic probability $\rightarrow$ Triggers Impersonation Alert. |
| **Human Social Engineering** | Real human scammer poses as bank official demanding OTP/PIN. | XLM-RoBERTa Intent (`OTP_REQUEST`) + Social Engineering (`AUTHORITY`, `URGENCY`) $\rightarrow$ Escolates risk to CRITICAL regardless of human voice. |
| **Adversarial Audio Perturbation** | Attacker adds subtle noise/jitter to trick deepfake detector. | Multi-evidence fusion (STT intent + speaker anomaly) prevents bypass; training incorporates heavy telecom noise augmentation. |
| **Replay Attack** | Attacker replays genuine pre-recorded audio of target speaker. | Codec artifact analysis + spectro-temporal phase continuity detection + intent verification. |

---

## 2. Attack Lab Non-Covert Security Controls

- **Watermark & Provenance Tagging**: All synthetic audio generated by Attack Lab embeds a cryptographic metadata header (`is_synthetic: true`, `generator_family`, `consent_hash`).
- **Consent Gate**: Generation requires passing a valid reference consent token associated with registered research profiles.
- **Decoupled Architecture**: Defensive engine does not expose generation APIs.
